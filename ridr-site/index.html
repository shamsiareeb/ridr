How it works

Three steps, no dispatcher

No surge pricing, no algorithm deciding your fare. Just people going the same way.

1

Post or search

Drivers list empty seats on a route and date. Riders search by where they're headed.

2

Request a seat

Send a request with how many seats you need. Drivers accept or decline in the app.

3

Chat and go

Confirm pickup details over in-app messaging, then hit the road together.
